## SFX / breaths / laughter (Hieu 22:05 + 22:19)
Spontaneous per character + scene — tables are DEFAULTS only, never stamps. Giggles (ふふっ/うふふ/んふふ) are LAUGHTER (e.g. Paulette "Fufu"/"Ufufu"), NEVER gasps ("Ư hự"). "Don't lose the sound" still holds (every JP sound must have a VN rendering). Default to staying as close to Japanese as possible; switch to pure Vietnamese ONLY when the Japanese-faithful rendering sounds too awkward. Vowel-length formula: VN vowel count = 1 + number of う/ぅ in JP. っ → Ư with matching punctuation; sentence-final emphatic っ attached to a CONTENT word is NOT a gasp (never add Ư — e.g. ブロワーっ); BUT っ attached to a SOUND-word IS a gasp → add Ư (ああっ, はうっ — decided 20/11; "don't lose the sound" rule). GASP BOUNDARY (locked 20/11): add Ư ONLY when the っ sound would otherwise be COMPLETELY LOST (absolute-final with no punctuation after, or line-initial); NEVER double-render an already-rendered っ (あっ――→"A——", ふあっ――→"Phu a——", ちゅっ→"Chụt", んっ→"Ưm", action SFX like ごちゅっ→"Phập") — position doesn't matter, survival of the sound does; precedent ハチロク01 2319 んあーんっ (baby moan, cut-off already conveyed by final "n" + period, TW ends 嗯) → NO Ư; っ inside words and already-rendered SFX excluded. ひあ/ひゃ family (ひあっ, ひゃっ, ひゃわっ, ひゃあ, etc.) is SPONTANEOUS per character + context (Hieu 20/11 — use freely); do NOT unify Hya/Hia/Á/Hyawa/Kua/Hự/hự/hây into one form. ROMAJI FALLBACK FOR MOANS (Hieu approved 21/11 ~01:56): when a Vietnamese rendering of a moan/breath sounds ngượng (forced/cringey) — especially long multi-vowel moans and the fa/fu family ("phà"/"phùa") and ひあ/ひゃ family — use the romaji of the JP sound instead (Hepburn, matching JP sound shape; vowel length per the vowel formula), e.g. ふあぁ→"Fuaa", ひゃぁ→"Hyaa". One row = one rendering (Vietnamese OR romaji, never mixed). Ư for lost gasps STAYS (short, natural, 1,146 rows already fixed — do not revisit). Applies to unaudited files going forward + re-examination in the unification pass for already-merged fa/fu and ひあ/ひゃ rows.
